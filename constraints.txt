# stack in pyproject.toml, and mujoco and viser here agree with it. numpy is the
# 2.x the twins were measured with; pin 4.x needs numpy>=2. The macOS
# exceptions below are compatibility pins, not the paper's measurement stack.

# see docs/contributing.md for the isolated CPU setup and validation.
